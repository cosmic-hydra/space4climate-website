Rebranded as Space4Climate

Workshop-based learning that connects space and climate.

Space4Climate is a hands-on learning experience where young people explore real-world climate challenges through the excitement of space science, teamwork, and practical activities.

Hands-on workshops

Build, test, and experiment through activities designed to make climate and space science tangible and memorable.

Space meets climate

Use ideas from satellites, Earth observation, and exploration to understand climate systems and local impact.

Built for young learners

Accessible, practical sessions that support curiosity, confidence, and problem-solving in schools and communities.

Action-oriented learning

Every session turns insight into action with collaborative challenges and creative solutions students can apply.
