Space4Climate.org

A fun, cool, online workshop, for schoolkids globally, about space and climate change using film & social-media.

Let children learn about climate action through space, creativity, storytelling and hands-on digital activities.

We provide a hyper-cool educational experience for school children.... worldwide!!! :-)

We use cutting-edge, online tools to deliver an out of this world ride. We explain the crucial role of space data to understand climate change, and ask children to create a story using film & social media.

Let them join us on a fun path of discovery!

  • engage kids in Climate Action in a fun way, with hands-on film-making activities.
  • involve parents, friends, family & community through social media.
  • increase their knowledge and motivate them to take care of the Planet.
  • let kids speak up about what they think is important.
  • give a chance to win once in a life-time prizes.
