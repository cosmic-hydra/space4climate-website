How it works

Everything you need to know about creating boards, marking your availability, and finding the perfect meeting time for your Space4Climate team.

Quick start 5 steps

1

🚀 Launch a board

On the Orbit Scheduler homepage, fill in the Board name (e.g. "Climate Kickoff"), choose a start & end date (up to 21 days), and set the time window your meetings might fall in. Click Launch board. The board is instantly created and stored in your browser.

2

👤 Enter your name

At the top of the board view, type your name into the Your name field and click Save. This links all the slots you click to your name, so the group view knows who's available when.

3

🗓 Mark your availability

The grid shows all the time slots: columns are dates, rows are hours. In Mark mode, click any slot to toggle it — it glows with a white ring when selected. You can also click and drag across multiple slots to add or remove a whole range in one gesture. Your selections are saved automatically.

4

📡 Invite your team

Click Share board and copy the link. Send it to your teammates. Anyone with the link can open the same shared board on any device, type their name, and mark their slots.

5

📤 Cloud sync & backup

Orbit Scheduler now stores each board in a public cloud JSON document, so cross-device updates sync through the shared board link automatically.

Normal workflow: Open the board link → enter your name → mark your availability → wait a moment for the board to sync.

Backup workflow: If someone was offline, click Export my response to copy a backup code. The organiser can use Merge response to import it later.

Key features

🖱

Drag to select

Click and drag across any range of slots to mark or unmark them in one fluid motion. Works on touch screens too.

🌈

Live heatmap

The grid updates in real time as each participant adds their name and marks slots. Best times are ranked automatically in the sidebar.

🔒

Shared cloud board

Each board is stored in a public JSON document so GitHub Pages users can access the same schedule from any device without Firebase or accounts.

📤

Export & merge backup

Each participant can still export a compact backup code. The organiser can import these codes later if they need to recover or merge offline responses.

📅

Up to 21 days

Schedule across up to three weeks in a single board. Set any time window (e.g. 09:00–18:00) to keep the grid focused.

🌍

Timezone display

Your local timezone is shown on the board so everyone knows which timezone the times refer to. Ideal for international Space4Climate teams.

Frequently asked questions

Can I edit or remove my availability after saving?
Yes. Open the board, make sure your name is saved, and simply click (or drag) any slot to toggle it. Your changes are saved instantly. You can also click the ✕ Clear button next to your name in the Participants panel to remove all your selections at once.
What happens if I open the link on a different device or browser?
The shared board should load on any device that opens the link, and updates should appear after a short sync delay. If cloud access was interrupted, you can still use Export my response on one device and Merge response on another as a manual backup path.
How do I share a board with someone who's never visited this site?
Share the board link (click Share board → Copy). When they open it on their device they should see the same board immediately, enter their name, and mark their slots. Because the board is public, only share links for schedules you are comfortable exposing to anyone who receives the URL.
Can I have multiple participants on one device (e.g. in a classroom)?
Yes — this is the simplest workflow. Pass the device around, have each person type their name and save it, then mark their slots. Each saved name creates a separate participant entry. The heatmap builds up automatically as each person adds their availability.
Will my boards still be there next week?
Usually yes. The board is stored in a public cloud document and each browser also keeps a local cache for convenience. Clearing your browser storage may remove your saved name on that device, but the shared board itself should still be available from the link unless the cloud service removes it.
Where does the "best times" ranking come from?
The sidebar ranks time slots by how many participants marked themselves as available, from highest to lowest. The percentage shown is the share of all current participants who are free at that slot. Slots are listed individually (one hour each), so if you want a 2-hour block you can look for consecutive high-ranked slots.
How do I get a booking reference ID for payment?
Once the heatmap shows good availability, click a top "Best time" entry and confirm "Book". A short reference like S4C-7K9P2Q is instantly created and shown in the sidebar and board info. Share or note this ID so the teacher can quote it when paying Space4Climate for the session.
