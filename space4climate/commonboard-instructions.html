How it works

Everything you need to know about creating boards, marking your availability, and finding the perfect meeting time for your Space4Climate team.

Quick start 5 steps

1

🚀 Launch a board

On the Orbit Scheduler homepage, fill in the Board name (e.g. "Climate Kickoff"), choose a start & end date (up to 21 days), set the time window, and pick a slot size (15, 30 or 60 minutes). Click Launch board and you'll get a unique link to share with your team.

2

🔗 Share the link

Click Share board and send the link to your teammates. Anyone who opens it — on any device — sees the same board. As people mark their availability, the grid updates live for everyone, so there's nothing to email back and forth.

3

👤 Sign in

Type your name in the sign-in bar and click Sign in. You can add an optional password to lock your row, so only you can edit your availability. Signing in again later with the same name (and password) lets you pick up where you left off, even from another device.

4

🗓 Paint your availability

The left grid is Your availability: columns are dates, rows are time slots. Click any slot to toggle it green, or click and drag across a range to fill it in one gesture (works on touch screens too). The right grid, Group's availability, fills in automatically as everyone paints.

5

🌍 Find the best time

The Group's availability grid lights up brightest where the most people are free, and the Best times panel ranks the top slots automatically. Hover any cell to see exactly who is free and who is busy.

No cloud configured? The board still works in local mode on a shared device — or each person clicks Export and sends their code to the organiser, who clicks Merge to combine everyone.

Reading the heatmap

The grid cells are colour-coded by how many participants are available at that time. The brighter the cell, the more people are free.

Key features

🖱

Drag to select

Click and drag across any range of slots to mark or unmark them in one fluid motion. Works on touch screens too.

📡

Live, shared grid

When cloud sync is on, everyone with the link works on one board. As teammates paint their availability, the heatmap and best-times ranking update in real time across every device.

🔐

Name + password sign-in

Sign in with just your name, or add an optional password to lock your row so only you can edit it. Sign back in later from any device to keep editing your availability.

📤

Local-mode backup

No cloud configured? The board still runs locally in your browser. Each person can Export a compact response code, and the organiser Merges them to build the full group picture.

📅

Up to 21 days

Schedule across up to three weeks in a single board. Set any time window (e.g. 09:00–18:00) to keep the grid focused.

🌍

Timezone display

Your local timezone is shown on the board so everyone knows which timezone the times refer to. Ideal for international Space4Climate teams.

Frequently asked questions

Can I edit or remove my availability after saving?
Yes. Open the board, make sure you're signed in, and simply click (or drag) any slot to toggle it. Your changes are saved instantly. You can also click the ✕ Clear button next to your name in the Participants panel to remove all your selections at once.
What happens if I open the link on a different device or browser?
With cloud sync on, it just works: the board and everyone's availability load from the cloud, so any device shows the same live grid. Sign in with your name (and password, if you set one) to keep editing your row. If the site is running in local mode instead, a different device starts empty — use Export on one device and Merge on the other.
How do I share a board with someone who's never visited this site?
Just send them the board link (click Share board → Copy). They open it, sign in with their name, and paint their slots — with cloud sync their availability appears on everyone's board instantly. In local mode, they'd Export their code and send it back to you to Merge.
Can I have multiple participants on one device (e.g. in a classroom)?
Yes — this is the simplest workflow without cloud sync. Have each person sign in with their name, paint their slots, then click Sign out so the next person can sign in. Each name creates a separate participant entry, and the heatmap builds up automatically as everyone adds their availability.
Will my boards still be there next week?
With cloud sync on, boards are saved in the cloud and persist for everyone until deleted. In local mode, the board lives in your browser's localStorage — it stays as long as you don't clear your browser's site data, so export important boards beforehand.
Where does the "best times" ranking come from?
The sidebar ranks time slots by how many participants marked themselves as available, from highest to lowest. The percentage shown is the share of all current participants who are free at that slot. Slots are listed individually (one hour each), so if you want a 2-hour block you can look for consecutive high-ranked slots.
