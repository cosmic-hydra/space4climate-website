Climate Services from Space

Trusted satellite intelligence for climate adaptation and resilience

Get in touch

Space4Climate connects Earth observation data with real-world climate action — helping governments, businesses, and communities access reliable climate intelligence from space.

Challenges

The challenge of climate intelligence

Satellite data is abundant, but transforming raw Earth observation into actionable climate intelligence requires specialist expertise and effective science communication.

Most organisations and communities are unaware of how satellite technologies already monitor emissions, track deforestation, measure sea level rise, and support climate adaptation decisions.

From finance to agriculture, industries struggle to integrate satellite-derived climate services into their operations, leaving critical data underutilised.

Traditional climate education does not reach enough young people with the depth and engagement needed to build long-term climate literacy and resilience.
