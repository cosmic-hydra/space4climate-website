What we value

Collaboration

Solving the climate crisis is a team effort. There are no individual successes; we either all win or all lose. Let’s be the winning team. 

Impact

The world urgently needs effective climate solutions. We need to mobilise resources intelligently, think flexibly, and work responsibly to realise them as quickly as possible. 

Optimism

What we’re doing is exciting, planetary-level stuff. It’s intensely complex and requires deep systemic change. We are daring to do the impossible for life. 

Integrity

If what we’re doing doesn’t work for climate, we’re doing it wrong. Affecting genuine systems-level change for good comes with immense responsibility. 

Community 

We are socially-driven and seek to lift each other up. We want to empower people to participate, grow, and thrive in the future we are building. 

Learning and growth

From structured learning opportunities and expert-driven training, to professional certification, conferences, and our annual MZT grant, Space4Climate is a place to realise your world-changing potential. 

Commitment to wellbeing

We value your health, happiness, and enrichment in and outside of work. Enjoy generous annual leave, unlimited mental health support, hybrid and flexible work policies, and private healthcare. 

Shared ownership of impact

People make companies. If you invest in our growth and success, you deserve to own a part of Space4Climate. Our Employee Share Option Plan offers you a meaningful stake in the business.

Our hiring process

Application review

Always reviewed by humans, never AI.

Interview with hiring manager (online)

A short call to learn more about your experience and expectations.

Skills and behavioural assessment (online) 

A multiple-choice questionnaire to help us learn more about how you work best. There are no right or wrong answers.

Case study assignment (in-person preferred) 

Your chance to get stuck into the subject matter of the role and discuss your ideas with us. Office and lab tours if held in person.

Final leadership interview (in-person preferred) 

Time with our founders to get to know each other, explore your ambitions, and cover any unanswered questions ahead of a potential offer.
