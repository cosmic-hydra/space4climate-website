How to Book a Slot

1

Browse the Timeline

Use the week navigation arrows to browse available sessions. The current week is shown by default, with today's column highlighted in blue.

2

Pick Your Slot

Click any white "Available" slot on the calendar. Slots in the past are greyed out. Each slot represents a one-hour session window.

3

Enter Your Details

Fill in your name, contact email or phone, and an optional note about your session topic or purpose. Then click "Book Slot" to confirm.

4

View or Cancel

Click any booked slot (shown in blue) to view its details. You can cancel the booking from the details view if plans change.

5

Share Your Availability

Switch to ✅ My Availability mode, enter your name, and click slots when you are free. Hit 📤 Export My Availability to get a shareable code, then send it to your team.

6

See Team Overlaps

In 👥 Team View mode, paste codes from team members using 📥 Import Team Member. The calendar highlights slots where multiple people are free, making it easy to find a shared time.
