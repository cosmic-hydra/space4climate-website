Community Guidelines

1

Be Respectful

Treat all community members with respect and kindness. We welcome diverse perspectives and encourage constructive dialogue about climate science and education.

2

Share Responsibly

When sharing resources, data, or information, ensure accuracy and cite your sources. Help maintain the scientific integrity of our community discussions.

3

Stay On Topic

Keep discussions relevant to climate science, space technology, and education. Help others by posting in the appropriate category and using descriptive titles.

4

Support Learning

We're all here to learn. Be patient with beginners, celebrate student achievements, and foster an environment where questions are welcomed and answered thoughtfully.

5

Collaborate Openly

Share your knowledge freely and collaborate generously. Our mission is to advance climate education, and we grow stronger when we work together.

6

Report Issues

If you encounter inappropriate content or behavior, please report it to our moderators. Help us maintain a safe and productive community for everyone.
