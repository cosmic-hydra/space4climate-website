Orbit Scheduler

A space-mission-grade group scheduling tool. Create a board, share the link, and your whole team marks when they're free on one shared, live grid — then watch the best meeting time light up.

🛰 Satellite-inspired 🌍 Climate-focused 📡 Live sync
🚀

Launch new board

Create a scheduling board for your team. Set the date range and time window, then share the unique link.

🌐

Join existing board

Got a board link or ID from a teammate? Open it here to load the shared schedule and add your availability.

How it works: Anyone who opens a board's link can sign in with their name and paint their availability. Everyone sees the same grid update live.
🚀
1. Launch
Create a board with your date range and time window.
📡
2. Share the link
Send the board link to your team. It works on any device.
👤
3. Sign in
Each person enters their name (and an optional password to lock their row).
🗓
4. Paint availability
Click or drag across the grid to mark when you're free.
🌍
5. Orbit found
The group heatmap highlights when the most people are free.
⚠️ Local mode. Cloud sync isn't configured, so this board lives only in this browser. It's perfect for a single shared device (pass it around the room), or use Export / Merge below for cross-device responses. To enable real cross-device, live scheduling, add your Firebase keys in firebase-config.js.
